# Holly Jones color palettes

Both Holly Jones sites offer five selectable color worlds, shown by name in a palette-icon menu. Selections persist independently per site. Both now open in Noir & Gilt; Heritage restores the original multicolor editorial look.

## Heritage

The original editorial mix of aubergine, forest, burgundy, blush, sage, and deep teal section gradients, with the cream, charcoal, and antique-gold brand colors.

## Noir & Gilt

Black `#090909`, porcelain white `#F8F6F0`, and antique gold `#B09152`.

## Velvet Violet

Deep aubergine `#211936`, orchid `#D95A9A`, and pale lilac `#F3EAF7`.

## Pacific Tide

Ocean blue `#083D52`, warm coral `#F0785D`, and clear aqua `#E1F3F5`.

## Citrus Grove

Botanical green `#264E36`, bright citron `#C5DF59`, and soft citrus cream `#F3F4D9`.
